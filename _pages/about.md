I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the University of Delaware, specializing in international trade, applied microeconomics, and development economics. My research examines how trade policy and institutional constraints shape firm behavior in developing economies, with a particular focus on how firms reallocate resources, adjust inputs, and respond to policy shocks.

Outside of research, I enjoy going to the gym and staying active. When I'm not working with data or writing, you can usually find me training or exploring new AI tools and technologies.

For more details about my research,  [CV](/files/umer_resume.pdf)., and ongoing projects, please explore my site or reach out at umersoha@udel.edu.
